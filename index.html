Vermont Forest Business School · Est. 2020

Building Better Businesses Across Vermont's Working Forests

Vermont Forest Business School helps working landscape professionals strengthen the business side of their work. From forestry, logging, sawmilling and firewood to maple sugaring, invasive species control and trail building, FBS offers practical education, useful resources and opportunities to improve how businesses are managed.

Since 2020, its Vermont programs have brought together working professionals to consider the decisions that shape profitability, resilience and long-term viability. With the last Vermont-only six-month Essentials cohort now underway, the work continues through upcoming short courses, resources, applied projects and evolving forms of business assistance.

Our Vermont track record

Since 2020, in Vermont

The Vermont Forest Business School began in 2020 with a simple format: a Vermont-only, six-month Winter Essentials program for working lands business owners. Each year, a small cohort of serious people gathered weekly to sharpen the business side of their work.

It worked. So well, in fact, that it grew into regional programs and now a national version.

Weekly sessions were real working conversations — Socratic discussions that force you to think and apply, not just listen — supported by audiobooks, podcasts, and notes.

The final Vermont-only six-month Essentials cohort is underway, October 2026 through March 2027.
